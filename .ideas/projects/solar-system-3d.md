# 3D model of Airel's solar system (Threlte + Blender)

Handoff notes for picking back up the animated 3D model of Airel's two suns and
their planets. Written from a search of every branch of this repo and of
scottBowles' other GitHub repos (Nov 2026). The original work stopped in Aug 2024.

### What's only on the user's machine

- **Blender `.blend` files.** None are committed anywhere. Every planet `.glb` in
  `solars` was exported with "Khronos glTF Blender I/O v4.1.63" (Blender 4.1), with
  baked diffuse/normal/roughness textures (e.g. `Janus_diffuse`, `Magmus_normal`).
  To change a planet's look, you need the `.blend` and re-bake/re-export.
- **The local checkout of `solars`.** It was cloned into a folder named
  `~/Dev/threlte` (the generated components say
  `npx @threlte/gltf@2.0.3 /Users/scottbowles/Dev/threlte/static/models/...`, and
  `package.json`'s `name` is `"threlte"`). It may have uncommitted work newer than
  Aug 27, 2024.
- **`threejs-playground/big_files/`** is gitignored ("Move big files to a gitignored
  folder"): probably the 2k/4k Sketchfab originals.
- **Sketchfab downloads**: the suns (`RedSun.glb`, `YellowStar.glb`) and the early
  `LavaPlanet1k`, `PurplePlanet*`, `GreenPlanet1k` came from Sketchfab, not Blender.
  Check their licences before shipping them in Airel.

Things to look for on the machine: `~/Dev/threlte` (run `git status` there),
`~/Dev/threejs-playground/big_files`, and any `*.blend` files
(`mdfind -name .blend` on macOS).

## The `solars` app

Stack (2024): SvelteKit 2, **Svelte 4**, `@threlte/core` 7, `@threlte/extras` 8,
three 0.159, `svelte-tweakpane-ui` for live tweak panels, `adapter-vercel`, pnpm.
Routes: `/` is the full system, `/demo` is one planet on its own for tuning shaders.

Key files:

- `src/lib/constants.ts`: sun positions (yellow at `(5,0,0)`, red at `(-5,0,0)`)
  and the `PLANETS` array (name, scale, orbitSpeed, sun, semiMajorAxis,
  eccentricity, inclination).
- `src/lib/components/Scene.svelte`: camera + OrbitControls, `<Stars />`, both suns
  with point lights, a `Planet` and an `OrbitLine` per planet, and Chromium.
- `src/lib/components/Planet.svelte`: moves a planet along its ellipse with
  `useTask`. Click focuses the camera on it (tweened); Escape goes back. **Right now
  every planet renders the same model (`Janus2`)** as a test: the per-planet model
  switch is commented out at the bottom of the file.
- `src/lib/components/PlanetTidalLock.svelte`: same, but keeps one face toward its
  sun (made for Janus). Not wired into `Scene` yet.
- `src/lib/components/Chromium.svelte`: Chromium flies a **figure-eight between the
  two suns**, switching suns every period; maths in `getFigureEightPositionForAngle`
  in `src/lib/utils.ts` (unit tests in `utils.test.js`). It's still a plain red sphere.
- `src/lib/components/Clouds.svelte`, `Atmosphere.svelte`: custom shader shells
  (simplex-noise FBM clouds; fresnel atmosphere). Uniforms come from context stores
  in `src/lib/stores.ts` and are tweakable in the Tweakpane on the page.
- `src/lib/components/models/*.svelte`: generated by `@threlte/gltf` from
  `static/models/*.glb` via `pnpm model-pipeline:run`
  (`scripts/model-pipeline.js`).

Models in `static/models` (94 MB total, committed directly, not LFS):

- Named Blender planets: `Magmus`, `Falucia`, `Hyangwan`, `Janus`, `Janus2`,
  `Genussa`, `Mystra`, `Mystra2`, `Hielo` (each ~1–5 MB, one `Planet` mesh + baked textures).
- Blender experiments: `BlenderPlanet*` (planet + cloud + atmosphere meshes),
  `Planet3*`, `Planet4_*` (eevee vs cycles bakes). `Planet3.glb` and
  `Planet3_eevee.glb` are 232-byte empty Draco exports.
- Sketchfab: `RedSun`, `YellowStar`, `LavaPlanet1k`, `GreenPlanet1k`,
  `PurplePlanet1k`, `PurplePlanet2k` (24 MB).
- `threlte.glb`: the template's demo cube.

against 0.1 for the rest, left over from an older model.

## How it connects to Airel

- Lore: `api/place/models/place.py` has `Place.place_type` with `STAR`, `PLANET`,
  `MOON`, and `parent` (a planet's parent is its sun). So the real list of suns and
  planets, and which sun each planet orbits, is in the database, not hard-coded. The
  campaign's first log, the "On the Planets" world primer, describes the worlds.
- The web app already has a 2D **Star Chart** (SVG):
  `web/src/lib/chronicle/starChart.ts` and
  `web/src/lib/components/chronicle/StarChartView.svelte`. It groups planets by
  sun from `Place.parent` and colours a sun by its name ("The Red Sun", "The Yellow
  Sun"). A 3D view could use the same data.
- `web/` is now **Svelte 5 with runes** and the Vite+ toolchain (read `web/AGENTS.md`).
  The `solars` code is Svelte 4 (`export let`, `$:`, stores, `on:click`), and Threlte 7
  is Svelte 4 only. Bringing it into `web/` means moving to **Threlte 8** (Svelte 5),
  whose API differs (`oncreate`, `bind:ref`, `useTask` options, generated components
  written for Svelte 5). Regenerate the model components with a current
  `@threlte/gltf`, not 2.0.3.
- 94 MB of models shouldn't go into `web/static` as is. Choose the final set of
  models, compress them (`@threlte/gltf --transform` / gltf-transform: Draco or
  meshopt + KTX2/WebP textures), and maybe host them outside the repo.
