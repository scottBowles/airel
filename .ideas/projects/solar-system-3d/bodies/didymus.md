# Didymus: lore brief for the texture and pins

Twin planets around the Red Sun. DB: `Didymus` (Place:20, `UGxhY2U6MjA=`, PLANET, parent The Red Sun) with one child
region, `Didymus Prime` (Place:42), which holds every place in the DB. The other twin has no DB entry; the primer calls it
plain "Didymus". Every place below is on **Didymus Prime**. Nothing in the canon is set on the other twin.

## 1. Canon summary

- **Twin halves of one planet.** "Twin Planets. Used to be one, but was broken into two that kind of orbit each other.
  Didymus and Didymus Prime" (DB notes; On the Planets, 2013-01-01).
- **Two stories of the break:**
  - Popular theory: "reigning theory (by like a 60% margin) is that some kind of meteorite hit this planet. Small planet
    to begin with, and somehow was hit evenly enough that a stasis could come about. But many minority opinions, even that
    there was never one planet" (DB notes).
  - What the party saw in a vision of the Pantheon Wars: the Solar Cannon's "test shot … after an exodus from the planet
    closest to the red sun (Didymus), they blow it up and are satisfied by the results" (Iconic Conflicts, 2021-01-18).
    That makes the break a weapon strike and the cataclysm ancient.
- **Scorched since the break:** "Since the cataclysm that separated Didymus, the place is burned pretty deep" (Didymus
  Prime, 2019-08-22).
- **Desert, little water:** "Lots of sand. Very few living waters, so life is gathered around them, and Beduin life
  elsewhere" (DB notes). "Prices are dirt cheap, because it's mostly dirt. Little dustbowl. Armpit between the suns"
  (Didymus Prime, 2019-08-22). A local asks, mocking, "how do you get water out of a rock?" (same log).
- **Poor, empty, forgotten:** "they don't really have natural resources" (DB notes); "No one mines on Didymus"; it exports
  only "minerals that are beneficial to mix with things from other worlds" (Didymus Prime). "Where people hide their
  businesses … Every call center in the 'verse. Warehouses and mom & pop shops" (same log). "So sparsely and locally
  populated" (Reconstructing Nora, 2019-07-11). "Forgotten step-child of Genussa"; drug cartels; "people tend to go
  missing" (DB notes). The dragon's-blood drug trail "stalls somewhere in Didymus" (On the Planets).
- **People:** "tribal nomadic peoples, a lot of mudders", "small town West Virginia folk", "A little post-apocalyptic …
  Resourceful mad-max-y" (Didymus Prime). Natives and "colonials": "the tribes on Didymus … they've got their pockets all
  over the place moving around" (same log). Minotaur biker gang (the One Horn Riders), goblins, kobolds.
- **Whidmore** "owns more than half of Prime" (Off the Grid, 2019-08-01) and "is still buying up land, looking for a way
  to terraform and turn it into agriculture" (Didymus Prime). Locals mostly eat synthetic food.
- **Death world:** Klutch: "Didymus is my home where the goddess of death dwells and decay is an inevitability for all
  organic substances" (Minotaur Interrogation, 2019-11-07). Izar: "It's the closest we have to badlands/death"
  (Reconstructing Nora). The Labyrinth is "a maze of death" (Klutch, Minotaur Interrogation).

## 2. Visual cues

**Stated**
- Colour and light: "Closest planet to the Red Sun. There's a hazy crimson hue at all times. The Yellow Sun hangs in the
  sky, but it is dwarfed by the Red. It is uncomfortably warm" (Didymus Prime, 10 am on the surface).
- Surface: sand, dirt, dust ("a cloud of dust blankets the area"), "dustbowl", rock, an arroyo (dry wash) that ends at a
  storm drain. Gravel roads with potholes (Minotaur Motorcycle Chase, 2020-08-27). "Burned pretty deep" since the cataclysm.
- Water: "very few living waters"; life clusters around them.
- Size: "Small planet to begin with" (DB notes). Each twin is therefore small.
- Orbit: around the Red Sun (DB parent). "Closest planet to the Red Sun" (Didymus Prime; repeated in Iconic Conflicts).
  The twins "kind of orbit each other" in a "stasis" (DB notes).
- In orbit: "the Confederation orbiting station is fairly undermanned" (Didymus Prime). The party's ship parked in
  "geosynchronous orbit" (same log).
- Night side: small towns, warehouses, call centres, trailers. Goshen is "the biggest city on the planet".
- Moons: none mentioned.

**Inferred (mine)**
- Base palette: rust, ochre and tan sand and dust, under a red-sun cast. Darker scorched and ashy ground near the break
  ("burned pretty deep", "Ashtown").
- The two halves should look like one geology: the same sand and rock, cut open. An even split ("hit evenly enough")
  suggests twins of about the same size.
- Fractured faces: each twin should show a wide, dark, scarred face (exposed rock, old melt, cliffs) toward the other. The
  text never describes this face; the user's asset note asks for it.
- Tidal lock: a close pair "in stasis" would be tidally locked to each other, so each fracture face always points at its
  twin. I've assumed this for the layout below.
- Thin, dusty atmosphere with a faint red-brown haze at the limb. No cloud deck; at most small dust storms.
- Water: a handful of small oases (dark green and blue specks), each with a settlement. Goshen should sit at the biggest one.
- Whidmore's terraforming: a few geometric green farm patches on Prime. These are an inference from "turn it into
  agriculture" and "fresh foods".
- Night lights: very sparse. One brighter cluster (Goshen), a few dim specks.

## 5. Contradictions and open questions for the DM

1. **Which twin is bigger, and what does each look like?** Is "Prime" the larger one? Is the other twin ("Didymus")
   inhabited, and does it differ at all (more sand? more water? more scorched)?
2. **Shape:** two round worlds each with a scarred face, or visibly broken chunks (half-spheres)? How far apart are they?
   Close enough to fill each other's sky?
3. **Do people cross between the twins?** Shuttles, or a debris bridge? Klutch is "from a backwater town on Didymus":
   the twin, or the system?
4. **Cause of the break:** keep the meteorite theory as the public story and the Solar Cannon strike (Iconic Conflicts) as
   the truth? Should the fracture face show a weapon scar, such as a glassed crater?
5. **Daedalus Headquarters:** is it Tom Tom's lot in Ashtown (as the logs read), or a separate site? If it's Tom Tom's
   lot, should its DB parent be Ashtown?
6. **Where is the One Horn Riders' compound** (the Labyrinth's entrance): in or near Goshen, or near Ashtown?
7. **"Closest planet to the Red Sun" versus "Armpit between the suns":** is Didymus the innermost Red-Sun planet? Does its
   orbit put it between the two suns?
