## 1. Canon summary

**Origin**
- After the Desconnegut (flown under the false ID "CSS Cookiebuttons") was wrecked, Daryo "Mr. Worlds" offers a new ship: "Made to avoid a punch rather than give one." It ran on "Archus Ultra PCU… a conversion of kinetic and photon energy". He offers to "retrofit it to go on Gallium… They'd be isolating jets, giving a little more maneuverability. You could flip one, keep the other going, and she could spin you in circles." Result: "a ship that runs twice as fast as the old one and maneuvers twice as well. Also twice the PCU." The name list ends with "Kontularien (story tellers)" (*Daryo Mr. Worlds*, 2019-05-23).
- "We dub our new ship the Kontularien ('Storytellers' in Basque). And we take off!" (*Reconstructing Nora*, 2019-07-11)

**Specs** (artifact notes; Starfinder rules)
- Sensors: "Scanner jammers and mid-range sensory array (10 hex range)".
- Weapons:
  - "Forward Facing Ion Cannon 2d8"
  - "Plasma Turret with EMP 3d8 fire or EMP with 6 hex range"
  - "rear-facing StoneFish MDS (mine deployment system) with 27 mines"
  - "Vortex Flechette Needler… (rear-facing)"
- Engine and handling: "Speed: 8", "Maneuverability: good (1)", "gallium retrofit isolating free turbine rig: (GRIFTR) PCU: 200".
- "Shields: 36 points (9 per side)", which means four facings.
- "Gemira's ConFederation Gate Pass Clearance Level G:9".
- The engine is a "polvimithe engine" (*Tide Pool Crawdads…*, 2023-12-02). Pure polvimithe dust thrown in the engine gives "ludicrous speed" (Desconnegut, *Escape Through the Gate*, 2019-05-02).

**Layout and rooms**
- "We convene in our common area, a deck below the bri[dg]e, and a deck above our quarters."
- "(We have only the two bays.)" Docking bays A and B, each with an airlock door and a viewport onto a corridor.
- "one of the turrets"; Izar "goes up to the bridge, getting ready to get in a turret". A "turret weapon station" is on the bridge.
- "Dorinda goes down to the cargo bay".
- Izar "comes down the stairs, running toward the bay"; Dorinda "dashes up the stairs toward engineering". "Sick Bay not far" from the docking-bay hallway.
- "hall lights"; "Death's Hourglass is (much) too far away in the galley".
- Kitchen: Hrothulf's. Darnit's dumbbells.
- A cooler in a cargo hold; "a freezer".
- (All from *Intruders*, 2021-05-24.)
- **Bridge:** "Atchi will put the call up on the big screen… 'on screen'" (*Reconstructing Nora*). "Atchi's station" (*Off the Grid*, 2019-08-01). Battle roles: captain, pilot, science officer, engineer, gunners (Desconnegut, *Space Battle*, 2019-04-25).
- **Kitchen and galley:** Hrothulf's kitchen. A "kitchen-forge" sword-making montage (*WINEPRESS*, 2020-09-14). A microwave (*Daryo's Bodily Death*, 2021-04-12). Hrothulf "in the kitchen, picks up his kitchen knives".

**The ReDream**
- Multiple "ReDream chairs" with "electrodes" and an "I/O".
- At the start of a session, "Dos and Tres drop needle on the usual track, and the fog machine kicks on", the track being "Dream Weaver".
- "The heads up display" shows the dreamer's imagery. Engineering "worked up a way to look into the ReDream, non-interactive".
- Inside it is a dream world, notably the New York Sour Grapes bar ("room for 130 people… bar 100 ft directly ahead… a stage"). There's a hotel "Do Not Disturb" sign "inside and outside the wall of the ReDream".
- (*The Naming of Kozmo*, 2021-06-21; *WINEPRESS*; *Ghost in the Sheev*, 2020-11-23; *Espresso at the Bank*, 2022-09-05)

**Ship AI (DarAI, later Kozmo)**
- "the whole ship has been taken over as an extension of the ReDream" (*Daryo's Chair, Kozmo's Kontalaria*, 2021-06-07).
- "the AI can project throughout the ship, but can only receive input through the speakers and googley eyes we've given it" (*The Naming of Kozmo*). Daryo's farewell plays "in hologram".

**Daryo's chair**
- A life-support wheelchair with screens ("His chair is twice as loud now, with all his devices going", *Off the Grid*). Later stripped: a console on the left arm and a hidden compartment (2021-06-07).

**Small craft and vehicles**
- "The shuttlecraft brought us back to the Kontalarian" (*Convincing Daryo*, 2020-08-31).
- "Our shuttle was loaded with two 4-wheelers" (one red) (*Visparo*, 2021-06-28).
- Daryo's van is used on Genussa: "We pull up to the ship in the dock area. We get out of the van and into the ship" (*A Deal with the Devil*, 2022-11-07). It isn't stated that the van is carried aboard.

**Flight modes**
- It can enter atmosphere and land or dock ("pull ship in atmo", "park the Delorean outside the city limits", or drop the crew and stay "in geosyncrenous orbit"; *Didymus Prime*, 2019-08-22).
- The Desconnegut "descends, the docking door opens, and we go in" (*Shellback Ceremony*, 2019-04-11).

**Crew besides the party**
- Atchi (Yuan-ti pilot).
- Dos, Tres, Cuatro (engineering).
- Scruffy.
- Other hands who "unionized" and quit; after that "Dos is piloting" (*Espresso at the Bank*).
- Hiare (batfey) aboard. The griffins are summoned, not stabled.

## 2. Visual cues

Stated:
- A small-to-mid armed transport, built for evasion ("avoid a punch rather than give one").
- Twin (or more) independently throttled "isolating jets": "flip one, keep the other going, and she could spin you in circles".
- A forward ion cannon in the nose, a plasma turret, and rear-facing mine launcher and needler.
- Two docking bays with airlocks. At least two turrets.
- Several decks: bridge at the top, then the common area, then quarters, plus a lower deck with the docking bays, cargo bay, sick bay and engineering reached by stairs.
- Can land in atmosphere.

Inferred:
- **Size:** the Desconnegut's enemy drone was "a tiny bit smaller than ours". Starfinder figures (PCU 200, 36 shields, speed 8) suggest a Small/Medium frame, roughly 30–60 m, about the size of the Millennium Falcon or Serenity.
- **Engines:** a glowing gallium/polvimithe exhaust, perhaps silvery-blue (gallium). The pure dust burns hotter.
- **Look:** Daryo's Genussan style (shades, Blue Coconut, retro-cool) suggests a sleek civilian runabout with hardpoints added, not a warship. "Storytellers" invites a painted name or mural on the hull.
- **Bridge view:** a wide main screen, pilot station forward, science and engineering consoles, and a captain's seat. The turret seats are reached from the bridge.
- **Fly-through use:** a cockpit or bridge-window frame with HUD panels fits the planned "ship's display" UI.
