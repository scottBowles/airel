# Mystra: lore brief for the texture and pins

"The pink Dagobah". A swampy, mystic world around the Red Sun. DB: `Mystra` (Place:17, `UGxhY2U6MTc=`, PLANET, parent
The Red Sun). **No DB place has Mystra as its parent.** `Tomb of Teresias` (Place:35) has no parent, but the planet's
notes put it on Mystra.

The party's adventures on Mystra (the tower-rocket launch, the Tomb, Ego's polvimithe) happened **before the first
session log** (2018-08-06). The logs only refer back to them, so the canon has almost no Mystra geography. Expect
mostly `guess` positions.

## 1. Canon summary

- "Planet of Scions and Mystics" (DB description). "Known for mystery, scionic happenings, place of mystics. Things might
  happen here that wouldn't elsewhere. Sort of a pilgrimage site" (DB notes; On the Planets, 2013-01-01).
- "**The pink Dagobah**. **Very swampy**" (same).
- "They were unaware of other planets until recently (through our actions)" (same). The party "flew a rocket off Mystra"
  (Parlay with the Drow, 2021-11-15). "The rocket went off that revealed space to the Mystrans" (Adventures at Meagel
  University, 2019-05-09). The rocket was "Thorin's rocket that once was a tower", which carried "the map" of the planets
  (Shellback Ceremony, 2019-04-11).
- "The ancient Netherese are here. Sort of weird ancient-technology relationship" (DB notes). "A couple netherese pop
  out of the mystical planet and people lose their mind" (Visparo, 2021-06-28). Races: "ancient Netherese, humans,
  dwarves, etc." (DB notes).
- "The Lady Saharel (the Blue Lady) and the Tomb of Teresias are both there" (DB notes). Marauders on the planet when the
  rocket launched "are said to have taken one of their treasures. Some blue lady. I think it's a diamond or something.
  Something they ask questions to" (Meagel University).
- "Nuns and lavender whiskey" (DB notes). The party carried cases of "Lavender Whiskey" (Exhuming Nora Omundsen,
  2019-06-20; Intruders, 2021-05-24).
- Politics: "House Lukesea as ambassadors" (DB notes). "The inhabitants who are a haughty people, the Loukasins"
  (Pilgrimage on Durinfol, 2020-11-09). Under Confederation lockdown: "Too bad that planet's closed down for the next year
  at least" (Meagel University). "The pilgrimages have largely stalled at the Confederate orbiting station … Lots of
  political upheaval in the Mystran sector" (Pilgrimage on Durinfol). There are the "Mystra Accords" (Monkey Lips at the
  Orange Star, 2022-11-21).
- Since the gods began to wake: "the place everyone is headed toward these days! Mystra! … A place thriving with life
  and energy" (Durinfol acolyte, Pilgrimage on Durinfol).
- Janus "has a similar feel as Mystra" (On the Planets).

## 2. Visual cues

**Stated**
- Pink. Swamp: "the pink Dagobah", "Very swampy".
- "Thriving with life and energy" (Pilgrimage on Durinfol).
- Orbit: around the Red Sun (DB parent). Didymus is "closest planet to the Red Sun" (Didymus Prime, 2019-08-22), so
  Mystra orbits farther out. Its place in order among Janus and Genussa isn't stated.
- In orbit: a "Confederate orbiting station" where pilgrims wait (Pilgrimage on Durinfol).
- Size and moons: not stated.

**Inferred (mine)**
- Dagobah means a dim, misty swamp: low wetlands, meandering channels, shallow pools, dense tangled vegetation, ground
  fog. In pink: magenta, rose and mauve vegetation, with murky violet-brown water.
- **Heavy, persistent mist or cloud cover.** The Mystrans didn't know other planets existed until the party's rocket,
  yet two suns and the other planets light up this system's sky. A sky that's always hidden explains that best. I propose
  a thick soft-white or pale-pink haze deck with gaps, which also fits "mystic". (DM question 1.)
- Lavender fields and currant vineyards: lavender whiskey; "the currants in the artwork on Mystra" (Disturbed Slumber,
  2024-09-09). These suggest some drier cultivated uplands in purple tones.
- Night side: few lights. A pre-spaceflight society with pockets of ancient tech. Maybe faint bioluminescent magenta in
  the swamps; the robots from Mystra glow "a sort of magenta" (Arcsubs, Robots, and Fog Clouds, 2018-10-16).
- The assets note already calls for a "pink swamp world, misty clouds". The reference image (pink with pale cream
  blotches) is mood only.

## 5. Contradictions and open questions for the DM

1. **Sky:** did Mystra have permanent mist or cloud cover hiding the suns and planets? That would explain the Mystrans
   not knowing about other worlds. If not, why didn't they know?
2. **How much is swamp?** All of it, or swamps with some uplands, seas or forests? Is pink the vegetation, the water, the
   mist, or all three?
3. **Where are the Tomb of Teresias, Thorin's tower-rocket and the complex, and the Blue Lady's shrine** relative to each
   other? Are any of them the same site? Should the Tomb's DB parent be Mystra?
4. **"Thorin":** a person (Netherese or dwarf), or a slip for Thiton?
5. **Is the Blue Lady still on Mystra**, or was she taken by the marauders?
6. **Size, moons and orbit order** among the Red Sun planets (Didymus is innermost)?
