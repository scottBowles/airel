# Open questions for the DM, by how much they change the models

Collected from the briefs in this folder (each brief has its full list). Each question has the
default used if it isn't answered.

## Shapes the whole look of a body

1. **Terran: seas, or city everywhere?** Default: city across all land, with a few seas and
   lakes, parks as green patches, and urban or icy poles.
2. **Felucia: how much ocean?** Default: mostly jungle with small seas (as in the reference
   image), and one large wildlands region.
3. **Hyang Wan: islands only?** Default: islands only (grey-green under constant grey rain cloud),
   about 10% land, no polar ice.
4. **Janus:**
   - Which side is dark from the butte? Default: west, as the two later logs say.
   - Is it dark from tidal lock, cloud, or both? Default: both: tidal lock to the Red Sun, plus
     geyser-steam cloud over the dark side.
   - What is the light side like? Default: scorched and arid.
   - Is there ice? Default: none.
5. **Durinfol: icy or temperate?** Default: temperate, per the log: a blue-green saltwater coral
   lake, green shores, small polar caps.
6. **Gonhirrim moons:**
   - Atmospheres? Default: all have thin air and weather.
   - Sizes? Default: similar sizes, with Durinfol slightly bigger.
   - Tidal lock? Default: yes, tidally locked.
7. **Gonhirrim's colour.** Default: the reference image's pink, orange and yellow bands, with one
   great storm.
8. **Didymus:**
   - Sizes? Default: Prime larger.
   - Shape? Default: two round worlds, each with a scorched, glassed fracture face from the Solar
     Cannon strike.
   - Distance apart? Default: close enough to loom in each other's sky.
   - Is the other twin inhabited? Default: barely.
9. **Mystra:**
   - Mist hiding the sky? Default: permanent pink mist and cloud.
   - Swamp share? Default: mostly swamp, with some uplands.
   - Where does the pink come from? Default: vegetation and water.
10. **Hielo: shape?** Default: a round icy body, the largest in the belt.
11. **The belt's look?** Default: a mixed rock-and-ice arc between the suns.
12. **Chromium: is "dark" literal?** Default: literal: a near-black surface under storm cloud,
    with ember-lit fissures.

## Shapes the system and navigation

13. **Gates:**
    - Five named gates, or one wormhole per planet? Default: five named gates, each a pair of
      mouths linking two planets.
    - Which planets does each link? Default: a guess, except the Hyang Wan↔Genussa gate, which
      the logs show.
    - What do they look like? Default: a faint natural vortex with Confederate beacons.
14. **What does the belt and Hielo orbit?** Default: the Yellow Sun, slowly.
15. **Chromium's figure-eight:** how big, and through the belt? Default: a large figure-eight in
    the plane, crossing between the suns.
16. **Moons and rings:**
    - Hyang Wan ("the moon" sets over Mulgogi in two logs)? Default: one small moon.
    - Terran and Genussa (the sketch draws ellipses)? Default: none.
17. **Are the sketch's planet order and the sun sizes right?** Default: yes, the order 1–10 from
    Yellow to Red, and both suns the same size.
18. **Confederate stations:**
    - Model one at each planet? Default: yes, a small station near each planet.

## Places and pins

19. **Genussa's quadrants:**
    - Do they cover the whole planet or one city? Default: the whole planet, meeting at the
      river-mouth bay.
    - Is G3 anarchy (2019) or the clean business district (2022)? Default: 2022.
    - Is the Orange Star in G2 or G3? Default: G3, per the DB.
20. **Hyang Wan scale:**
    - Were the temple trips' hours by flight or by sea? Default: by flight.
    - Where is Hatatori? Default: about 125° west of Kamasu.
    - Pin Thiton's Temple as a ruin? Default: yes.
21. **Places with no known position** are pinned by guess unless you say where:
    - all of Felucia and Mystra;
    - Terran's Senate and Arena;
    - Didymus's One Horn Riders' compound;
    - Janus's House Roderick castle;
    - five on Magmus: Heorot, Kenaz, the Hot Springs of Canaz, Sinmara and Svalinn.
22. **Database parents missing**, so these places get no pins until they're set:
    - Gliten's Floating Cube Chapel → Broadbeam;
    - Lady's Ladle and the Colosseum of Mazoë → Ironfist;
    - Tomb of Teresias → Mystra;
    - Thiton's Temple → Hyang Wan;
    - Champion City → Terran (as a TOWN);
    - Dock A9 → Genussa.
    - There are also a few likely AI mis-links between Hielo and logs that never mention it.
