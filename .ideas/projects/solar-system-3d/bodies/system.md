# The Airel system — suns, orbits, layout, gates, travel

Canon is thin on numbers. Nothing gives a distance, a size or a period. Layout figures below are measured from the user's rough sketch (`../system-sketch.jpg`) and marked `layout`. The user says not to read much into it.

## 1. Canon summary

### The suns
- Yellow Sun `UGxhY2U6MQ==`: "Yellow refining fire, with the heat of purity". Red Sun `UGxhY2U6Mg==`: "Red wild fire, burning with the heat of passion". "In most mythologies they're understood as being related, whether as sisters or as husband and wife." (*On the Planets*, 2013-01-01; DB)
- Mythic origin: Gliten "cast them to the farthest distances, and they found balance as opposite lights (we see our galaxy's two suns). And the space between became the realms of peace and balance" (*Stories in Gliten's Chapel*, 2021-01-04).
- "Red Sun of Chaos and Yellow Sun of Law". Gliten spreads law and chaos "across the verse between the two suns… those who prefer structure to gravitate toward the yellow sun and the chaos to gravitate toward the red sun". The planets are "lava in water collected within the space between" (*Iconic Conflicts*, 2021-01-18).
- Seen from Didymus Prime: "Closest planet to the Red Sun. There's a hazy crimson hue at all times. The Yellow Sun hangs in the sky, but it is dwarfed by the Red." (*Didymus Prime*, 2019-08-22)
- The Vardum's Solar Cannon threatens "the destruction of the two suns" (*Raltak's Promise*, 2024-10-21). In the Pantheon Wars vision, a test shot blew up "the planet closest to the red sun (Didymus)", which explains its split (*Iconic Conflicts*).

### Orbits and membership
- DB parents: Yellow Sun has Magmus, Felucia, Terran, Hyang Wan, Gonhirrim (7 moons) and Hielo. Red Sun has Mystra, Genussa, Janus and Didymus. Chromium has no parent.
- Primer headings: "Yellow Sun Planets", "Red Sun Planets", "Both Suns: Chromium… figure-eight orbit around the two suns".
- Hielo: "In the asteroid belt, rather than orbiting a sun." The belt is "between the suns" (*Into the Black*, 2021-05-10). User ruling: a little nearer the Yellow Sun.
- Didymus: "Twin Planets… broken into two that kind of orbit each other". "Small planet to begin with." Didymus is the planet closest to the Red Sun (above).
- Gonhirrim: gas giant, "seven moons"; "'The eighth moon' is how they refer to the center of the gas giant".

### Gates (P.O.W.)
- Item **P.O.W. (phylotic orbital wormholes)** `SXRlbToz`: "Semi-organic interplanetary system of travel. Each planet has a naturally occurring wormhole within its orbital plane. All of these wormholes are interconnected through some kind of phylotic webbing. The philotes (subatomic rays of energy) of each planet have measurable frequencies. Attaching matter to these frequencies allows the master to travel through the wormhole to the adjoining phylotic connection and POW, you're in the Orbital Plane of that planet!"
- "There is mention of the gates, but they orbit in reverse orbit of the planet it's linked to (each is linked to a ship [sic, read: planet]). Confed ships have the ability to 'race to the gate' which is kind of a hyperdrive along the orbit -- the arc from planet to gate. Most private ships don't have this though, and have to be towed by a shuttle that has that capacity. Third-party racers also exist that you can put on your ship (illegally)." (*Into the Black*, 2021-05-10)
- Leaving Hyang Wan: "going through the gates would be faster anyway. 'We could get to Gonhirrim before then.'" The gate is "About ten minutes" away. "two Federation raiders outside of it"; "Four more ships have come through the gate" (*Space Battle*, 2019-04-25).
- Going through it: "we're about to miss the gate, or we're going to crash into it!" Confed: "You do not have clearance to enter that gate"; "the Starships and drones surround the gate". Then "we zoom through this gate… The world around us becomes a flash of light, and from this gate, we are now just outside the planet Genussa." (*Escape Through the Gate*, 2019-05-02). So this gate links the Yellow-Sun side (Hyang Wan) to the Red-Sun side (Genussa).
- Clearance: the Kontularien carries "Gemira's ConFederation Gate Pass Clearance Level G:9"; "Our G9 clearance gets us into atmo on Genussa" (*Espresso at the Bank*, 2022-09-05).
- The ship AI promises privacy "until we enter a gate (which they have made appear)" (*Daryo's Chair, Kozmo's Kontalaria*, 2021-06-07).
- **The names Vostok, Galileo, Tiberius, Lizhi and Aryabhata appear only in the sketch legend.** No log or DB entity uses them.

### Stations and networks
- Item **Confederate Orbiting Station** `SXRlbTo1`: "ConFed oversight orbiting planetary systems… think Space TSA, but more politically neutral. Orbiting Stations provide security checkpoints and Confederation oversight for interplanetary travel while providing sanctuary spaces for market, information, neutrality, and trade (halls of mint)."
- Gonhirrim: shopping "in one of the orbiting stations where there's higher quality (though also higher prices)"; "Typically one would need to stop by the orbiting station before entering" (*WINEPRESS*, 2020-09-14).
- Magmus: "an orbital Federation shuttle dock called Space Station Phoenix, which connects to an underground connection of tubes" (*Brawnheim Steamroller*, 2022-12-05). DB place COS Phoenix `UGxhY2U6Mjg=` (DISTRICT, parent Magmus).
- Ansible `SXRlbTo0`: "FTL phylotic communications… receivers at each Confederate Orbiting Station". Genussa "control the hub for it" (*On the Planets*). Ships use "Confed maps" over the ansible: "we could lose for a day and be fine" (*Reconstructing Nora*, 2019-07-11).
- A contact spends "lots of time between planets and in confederate orbiting stations" (*Shadows of Betrayal*, 2022-08-22).

## 2. Visual cues

- **Suns.**
  - Stated: yellow is "refining" and pure; red is "wild" and passionate.
  - User decision: similar sizes. The red one looks bigger in the sketch and from Didymus because Didymus is close to it.
  - Inferred: the shader suns in `web/src/lib/system/` already match (calm gold-white vs turbulent deep red).
- **Sky from the planets** (for cameras and lighting):
  - Magmus (a Yellow-Sun planet) sees both suns set together: "crystalline sand glistening in the setting suns" (*Bots, Bombs, and EMP Blasts*, 2023-05-15); "the suns will be in descent" (*The Floating Island*, 2023-04-24); "suns-down" (*Dixieland's Sausage Gravy Must Flow*, 2024-11-18).
  - Genussa: "The Red Sun begins to set" (*Manticore Green*, 2019-05-16).
  - Didymus: the crimson sky above.
- **Belt:** an arc of rocks between the suns, nearer the Yellow Sun, with Hielo its largest body. See `hielo.md`.
- **Gates:**
  - Nothing describes their look.
  - Stated: they're physical objects you can crash into, natural "semi-organic" wormholes "within the orbital plane", linked by "phylotic webbing", guarded by Confed ships and drones. The transit is a flash of light.
  - Inferred: a natural ring or vortex mouth with faint filament webbing toward its partner, plus Confed checkpoint craft. The sketch draws them as coloured diamonds; use those colours in the HUD.

**Gate mouths from the sketch.**
- There are 10 diamonds in 5 colours, each colour twice, all on a nearly straight line between the suns (about 0.03 D to one side).
- t is the position along the line from the Yellow Sun (0) to the Red Sun (1).

Proposal: treat each named gate as a **pair of mouths** (10 mouths in total). That fits the POW lore of a wormhole "in each planet's orbital plane", since 10 mouths could serve 10 bodies.
- Put each mouth on its planet's orbit, moving **retrograde** ("reverse orbit of the planet it's linked to").
- Show the "race to the gate" as a fast arc along the orbit from planet to mouth.
- This mapping is a `guess`.

## 4. Animation notes

- **Day/night:** planets rotate normally (sunsets on Magmus and Genussa; "It's about 10am" on Didymus). No day lengths are given anywhere.
- **Seasons:** Magmus has them ("sunbirds… In their migratory season now… southern migration", *Bets, Bites, and Baronies*, 2023-08-28). Nothing else.
- **Two suns together:** Magmus sees "setting suns" (plural), so from at least part of its orbit both suns stand close together in its sky.
- **Underground day cycle:** the Dao caverns on Magmus use lamps "mimicking the day cycle" (*Bad Habit Brawl*, 2024-04-01).
- **Janus / Mezzolux.** These clues affect whether Janus is simply tidally locked:
  - "One half's light, one half's dark, with a very little bit of overlap in the center" (primer).
  - "geysers of heated thermal vents… This is where the clouds come that cause the dark side to be dark, though pretty temperate" (*Visparo*, 2021-06-28). So cloud, not only lighting, makes the dark.
  - The thermal spurts "keep the dark side of the planet from being an ice-half-planet" (*Underground Exploration*, 2021-07-16).
  - The Mezzolux is narrow: "It would go pitch black maybe 20 miles to the west" of the butte (*Netting the Dagger*, 2022-07-11).
- **Direction conflict on Janus:** the butte entry (DB, *Gryphons to the Mezzolux*, 2022-01-31) says "darkness of the east and the light of the west". Two other logs say dark is **west** ("Heading due West (into the dark side)", 2021-07-16; "pitch black… to the west", 2022-07-11).
- **Didymus:** two halves that "kind of orbit each other".
- **Chromium:** figure-eight, switching suns each loop.
- **Gates:** counter-orbit their planets.

## 5. Contradictions and open questions for the DM

1. **Gate topology:** five named gates (sketch), or one wormhole per planet (POW item)? Is each named gate a pair of mouths linking two planets? Which planets does each link (for example, is Tiberius Hyang Wan↔Genussa)?
2. **Gate look:** a natural vortex or ring? Built structures around a natural wormhole? Visible at all without instruments ("which they have made appear")?
3. **Gate motion:** does "reverse orbit of the planet it's linked to" mean on the same orbit going the other way, so planet and gate meet twice a year?
4. **The belt and Hielo:** do they orbit the Yellow Sun, both suns, or neither? This decides whether the belt animates.
5. **Sun sizes:** confirm equal sizes despite the sketch and the Didymus sky.
6. **Distances:** is the sketch's order right (1→10 running from Yellow to Red)? Where is Hyang Wan (4)?
7. **Moons and rings:** do Terran and Genussa really have the ring/orbit ellipse and moon drawn on the sketch?
8. **Mythology slip:** *Iconic Conflicts* says "Yellow Sun of Law" and "Red Sun of Chaos", then gives yellow and red to "Gliton and Glitonea respectively", which reverses Gliton (chaos) and Glitonea (law). Which god goes with which sun?
9. **Stations:** does every planet have a Confederate Orbiting Station (implied by the ansible item)? Should each be modelled?
10. **Janus:** is the dark side east or west of the Mezzolux? Is the dark side made by tidal lock, by cloud, or both?
