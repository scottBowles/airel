# Hyang Wan: lore brief for the texture and pins

High-tech water planet of the Yellow Sun. DB: `Hyang Wan` (Place:7, `UGxhY2U6Nw==`, PLANET, parent The Yellow Sun;
aliases "Planet of Hyang Wan"). DB children: `Mulgogi` (region) → `Kamasu Complex` → `Bagmulgwan`; `Hatatori Island`
(region) → `JingLong Facility`. `Thiton's Temple` (Place:34) is on Hyang Wan by its description but has **no parent** in
the DB. Nearly all geography comes from one arc of 26 session logs (2018-08-06 to 2019-05-02), all set on or around
Mulgogi and the temple. **No ring** (user's ruling, assets.md).

## 1. Canon summary

- **Water world, always raining, high tech.** "Water planet. Technology. Constantly raining." (DB notes; On the Planets,
  2013-01-01). DB description: "High-Tech Water Planet".
- **People:** "Lots of Huan-ti and Hyangwanese. Locahaths, Kaminoans." (DB notes). On Mulgogi, "Yuan-ti largely inhabit
  the island" (Rickshaw Dan and the Kamasu Complex, 2018-08-06). Kaminoan and Yuan-ti scientists at Kamasu (Kamasu
  Complex, 2018-08-14). Hyangwanese has its own script and language ("ancient Hyang-Wanese", Bones, Crabs, and Castles,
  2019-01-29; the word for "friend" is written 출구, Crabs and Rock Operas, 2019-04-04). Place names are Korean-flavoured
  ("Bagmulgwan" is "Korean for 'museum'", DB).
- **Neutral, unpoliced, extremes of tech:** "Trade in tech, secrets, weapons and defense … Very neutral … Pretty well
  unpoliced. Wide swath of the super high tech and the super luddite, but the average crab farmer could have tech that
  could trap animals that could interdimensionally travel" (DB notes). Unrest: "overreaches of technological
  experimentation, and the guerilla warfare of a sort of the railman".
- **Two rival weapons plants on opposite sides of the planet.** Kamasu is "less concerned with confederation regulations
  than the weapons plant on the other side of the planet" (Rickshaw Dan…, 2018-08-06). The other is JingLong on Hatatori
  Island, "more federation legit" (DB). "The rival weapons facility--Jing Long on Hatatori Island--is outside of a major
  city" (Shellback Ceremony, 2019-04-11).
- **Giant stealthy squid migrate north each year**, causing tsunamis: "There is a squid migration that takes place at
  this time. It's kind of a big deal with the East Isles … Their path can be quite destructive, causing tsunamis. They
  are going north to warmer waters" — with a map of "the last 20 years of migrations, including the villages harmed,
  power outages" (Arcane Inquiries, 2018-08-28). They "match the temperature of the water" so can't be detected (When
  Demon Pigs Fly, 2018-09-04). Their breeding ground is tethered to the spire of Thiton's Temple (Thiton's Temple,
  2019-01-22).
- **Thiton's Temple**, an undersea cathedral of the god of water and life on a reef in open ocean, now **destroyed**: "The
  temple just crumbled" (Crabs and Rock Operas, 2019-04-04). Later called "the cathedral at Hyang Wan" (Pilgrimage on
  Durinfol, 2020-11-09; Durinfol has a separate, smaller Thiton temple). Temples "move" and differ per branch (Searching
  Dhund Hal Ka's Backpack, 2022-08-01).
- **A moon.** Erizo: "be in your cargo bay immediately when the moon goes down"; "The moon is in descent. We can see it out
  our window … about two hours before the moon breaks the plane of the horizon" (Arcane Inquiries, 2018-08-28); "twenty
  minutes before the moon falls" (When Demon Pigs Fly, 2018-09-04). No moon exists in the DB.
- **Leaving the planet:** a gate is ~10 minutes' flight out; "we're a couple hours from [the asteroid belt], and going
  through the gates would be faster anyway" (Space Battle, 2019-04-25). That gate delivered them "just outside the planet
  Genussa" (Escape Through the Gate, 2019-05-02).

## 2. Visual cues

**Stated**
- Rain everywhere, always: "Constantly raining" (DB). On Mulgogi: "Non-stop rain have blanched the landscape. Dull white.
  Everything is about the same color, about the same shade of green. Everything is greyed out." (Bagmulgwan,
  2018-08-07). "Still hasn't stopped raining" (Kamasu Complex, 2018-08-14). A room at Kamasu "all window out over the
  cliff, into the rain with the clouds" (Arcane Inquiries).
- Land on Mulgogi: trees/woods, an open valley, a bridge, waterfalls ("rain coming off the trees"), mud, and
  "landslides were a thing around here" (Escape from Kamasu Complex, 2018-10-23). A trench by the sea used as a junkyard,
  "that old crash site" (Arcane Inquiries). Underground: drainage conduits with "about nine inches of water", a "giant
  reservoir", a rail tunnel network (The Railman, 2018-11-13).
- Storms: lightning strikes the junkyard ("lightning down around the Complex, not coming from the sky", Bagmulgwan).
- Ocean: open sea with "a pretty strong current" around the temple; surface "swirling, teeming, bubbling mass of water,
  30 ft in diameter" over the squid (Unresolved Past and Divided Goals, 2019-01-15). The temple stands on a reef, spire
  top ~200 ft below the surface; algae and coral cover it (Thiton's Temple, 2019-01-22). North is "warmer waters".
- Equator: the crew held a Shellback (equator-crossing) ceremony on the flight to/from the temple (Shellback Ceremony).
- Night side / lights: high-tech society, "major city" by JingLong, Kamasu a "monolithic dome" powered by a rain-turbine
  (Kamasu Complex). Villages along the squid route lose power when hit.
- Moon: at least one, visible from the surface, sets below the horizon.
- Size, orbit: Yellow Sun planet, 4th in the primer's list (Magmus, Felucia, Terran, **Hyang Wan**, Gonhirrim, Hielo) and
  #4 on the user's sketch. A couple of hours' flight from the asteroid belt. No stated size or day length.

**Inferred (mine)**
- Surface: overwhelmingly ocean (well over 90 %); land as islands only — no continent is ever named ("the nearest large
  land mass" is an island). The creation myth has Gliton erupting "volcanoes … throughout the waters of Thiton, creating
  land masses in the midst of these oceans" (Stories in Gliten's Chapel, 2021-01-04): volcanic islands and archipelagos.
- Land colour: pale, washed grey-green, low contrast (the "blanched … greyed out" quote), not bright green.
- Ocean: deep teal/blue-grey; lighter turquoise only over reefs and shoals. Swirls (reference image) can come from
  currents and from the cloud deck.
- Clouds: a heavy, near-global rain deck (70–85 % cover), grey rather than white, with rain-band and cyclone swirls;
  the surface shows through gaps. Embedded lightning flashes are justified.
- Night side: city lights clustered on islands, softened by cloud; some unlit "luddite" islands. "Kaminoans" evokes
  Kamino (storm-lashed ocean, platform cities): optional sparse lit ocean platforms.

## 3. Places

Assumptions for distances: planet radius taken as Earth's (6,371 km, 1° ≈ 111 km). The temple travel times ("Closest
land is about five hours away by ship … Mulgogi is the closest large land mass, 8-10 hours away") are for the party's
starship, the Desconnegut, which flew there ("we have lift off"). I assume ~450 km/h low, in-rain flight, so 5 h ≈ 20°
and 8–10 h ≈ 32–40°. If the DM meant sea-going speed (~40 km/h) everything near the temple shrinks to a few degrees
(question 2). Longitude zero is arbitrary: I anchored Mulgogi at 24° W.

## 5. Contradictions and open questions for the DM

1. **Moon.** Two logs (2018-08-28, 2018-09-04) have "the moon" rising/setting over Mulgogi. Does Hyang Wan get a moon in
   the model (and a DB entry)? What is it like?
2. **Scale of the temple trip.** Were "5 hours" and "8–10 hours" flight times on the Desconnegut (→ temple ~35–40° from
   Mulgogi, as placed) or sailing times (→ a few degrees)? Is Hyang Wan about Earth-sized?
3. **Hatatori.** Placed ~125° west of Kamasu so that it is both "on the other side of the planet" and reached via Kamasu
   from the temple. OK? Is it bigger or smaller than Mulgogi, and does its major city have a name?
4. **East Isles.** Are these a named archipelago east of Mulgogi (on the squid route), or the whole eastern half of the
   planet's islands?
5. **Land.** Any continent or polar ice at all, or islands only? Roughly what share is land?
6. **Thiton's Temple.** It crumbled in 2019-04-04 and "temples move". Pin it as a ruin, or drop the pin? (DB: its
   `parent` is empty; should be Hyang Wan.)
7. "Islets … >½ mi in diameter": meant "<½ mi"?
8. Name of the east-coast town where the ship landed, and of the gate near Hyang Wan (Vostok/Galileo/…; it leads to Genussa)?
