The scene as it stands: bots encroaching seemingly nonstop, 3 bombs places and armed, obnoxious buzzing of the island that makes it difficult to hear, Darnit back in comms.

On the island, Ego is south almost in the middle. Hrothulf is due east. Izar is right behind Horthulf in the northeast. Dorinda is 60ft straight up. There remain two bots in front of Hrothulf on his way to the southeast, where the last bomb needs to be planted. Three bots are between that and Ego, plus the three trapped in a net. Of the three standing, two are repairing each other and the other is really bad off.

“Nilchi should be back any second to provide some health support any moment. It sounds like you’re under fire,” Darnit assures us.

Hrothulf asks Darnit about the containers that he thinks may be fuel. He says they are chemicals for terraforming. Corrosive, but no indication they are flammable.

Dorinda fires her sniper rifle at one of the bots between Hrothulf and the southwest corner. She’s really mastered the art of pinging them off the island. It is hit and pushed off and flails its arms cartoonishly to the ground. Hrothulf gives a primal yelp of approval in his rage. She and Sefarina move toward the antenna array and try to see whether anything looks particularly sabotage-able.

Hrothulf swings Watermelon’s Wail trying again to slash from nave to chops, but the bot sidesteps his enraged assault. Hrothulf comes back with a Mark McGuire baseball swing hoping to slash/knock him off, but misses once again.

Poyraz flies over and picks up one of the bots between the corner and Ego and throws it over the island.

Ego has Hayeta take her airbound over the bots and to the southeast corner. As they fly they hear a very small explosion coming from the tower to the southeast of this island. Ego looks over and there’s a very thin plume of smoke coming from under the roof of that tower. On reaching the corner she yells, “Yo Hrothulf! Bombs my way!” She runs to Hrothulf to grab the bomb, picking it up from behind Hrothulf. Hayete stays in the corner, taking a strong defensive position.

Izar runs by as Ego is picking up the bomb and says, “Ego, handoff!” ready to take the bomb like the Uruk-hai at Helm’s Deep. Ego sees the advantage and willingly gives a toss over the shoulder as Izar gives the Heisman post sprinting by, hair flowing with shadowy wisps in her wake. The sticky-bombed bots attempt attacks of opportunity as Izar runs by, but Izar evades handily. 

Fei flies to the southside.

As he begins setting the bomb, there is a cool wind from the east, as Nilchi arrives full of Healing Wind. Dorinda, over the comms, “Uh, you’re welcome.” We all say thanks.

Six bots have arrived on the south side of the island, two from each door, right on Izar. The two nearest Izar help eliminate the sticky residue from the stuck bots, so there are now five right nearby Izar.

Two of the bots charge at Hayete, riot shields unfolding from their forearms, But Hayete’s defensive position remains strong, digging her claws in and holding steady against this bullrush.

All around Dorinda little sparks erupt, a scattering flaschette from two of the bots on the island firing wildly. Dorinda and Sefarina are unhold and Sefarina maintains invisibility, though she is shaken.

Hayete sticks out her beak while fending off the bullrush a bot tries to run by. She beaks the bot, who ends up topping just a bit doing the toe-tap on the edge of the island then steps back in. Another manages to get by, but with Hayete’s attack neither is able to quite make it to Izar.

Izar is tackled and grappled, soon restrained by zip ties around his wrist, wrapping his hands behind his back. Fei attacks, but the bot shrugs off the damage.

Dorinda flies over to get a shot at the bot teetering on the edge. She not only knocks it over the edge, but somewhere inside she hits something, because as it’s falling over, it catches itself, then something in the body explodes and only the arms remain as the body falls to the ground. Then as soon as it hits the ground, it all goes to dust.

Sefarina flies in at the bot giving a tuskan raider celebration for having bound Izar, and she flips the bot over the edge. Dorinda dismounts, taking the Black Widow three-point landing.

Hrothulf rage-attacks the nearest bot between himself and Izar, wanting to make his way back to defending Izar. He takes a jedi-knight spin-and-slash with Watermelon’s Wail, and the bot is obliterated by the impact, bursting into flames in midair, then turning to dust, glistening in the suns-set. Hrothulf looks at the next bot and tries to look it in the eyes and give a wink, but the bot is too busy looking at the dust to notice. Hrothulf gives another spin-and-slash and this new bot is summarily decapitated. The head bounces off the caterpillar and back under its body just as its body descends into sand.

Poyraz comes over and does a hero’s landing next to Hrothulf and they pose menacingly awaiting more bots as Izar sets the bomb.

With Izar on the ground and Dorinda already ready to free Izar from the zip ties, Ego goes to the bomb and resumes setting it.

Hayete takes off straight into the air, taking attacks of opportunity from both bots who were bullrushing her. Hayete casts Shatter, with the five bots on the southside in her radius. One bot just collapses to the ground. The armor of the other four completely spider-webs with a crystalline shattering sound all over the place as their armor crackles all over their bodies.

Hiare goes to cut Izar’s zip tie off with Fei’s talon and Fei tries to cut it off, leaving it the zip tie shredded yet holding strong. Izar creates a ghostly shadowy hand and assails the zip tie with the chill of the grave, snapping it off with Chill Touch. For the next round Izar has a zombie zip tie familiar.
